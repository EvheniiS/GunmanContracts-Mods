[size=5][b]Heavy Melee[/b][/size]

A pistol is a heavy lump of metal. Now it hits like one. [b]Pistol-whip an enemy and they stagger[/b],
punches hit harder, and an enemy you've knocked down [b]feels every hit and stays down[/b].

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
In the game, swinging a gun at an enemy does nothing: no damage, no stagger. The knuckles of a hand
holding a gun don't count either. After your first punch knocks an enemy over, every hit on them does
[b]zero damage[/b] until they are back up, so you have to knock them down again and again.
[list]
[*][b]Weapon hits:[/b] hit an enemy with a pistol, rifle, shotgun or bow, or with the hand holding it,
and they stagger and take damage, like with the nunchucks.
[*][b]Harder punches:[/b] bare fists do twice the game's damage.
[*][b]Hits on the ground count:[/b] an enemy who is down or stunned takes damage from punches and
weapon hits.
[*][b]Keep them down:[/b] every hit on a downed enemy keeps them on the ground 2 seconds longer, and a hit
while they're getting up knocks them back down. Works with the nunchucks too.
[*][b]Knockout:[/b] on the ground, hits to any body part can finish them, not only the head or torso.
[*][b]No one-hit kills:[/b] one hit does at most half an enemy's health, so a head or torso takes two hits.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Grabbing still works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Open hand = no hit.[/b] Touch, push, grab a wrist or cover a mouth without knocking anyone over.
Squeeze grip to make a fist and punch.
[*][b]A fast open hand is a strike:[/b] a slap or a chop to the back at full swing (8 m/s) hits like a punch.
[*]The hand that's holding an enemy never counts as a punch, so a grab doesn't turn into a stagger, and the
game's shoulder throw works as before.
[*]Slow touches don't count: a punch needs 3 m/s, which a real punch easily reaches.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Also fixed[/b][/size]
An enemy lying on the ground used to get hurt by debris and objects they kicked around themselves (the game
counted a shard they pushed as a thrown object hitting them). That's gone. Things you throw at them still hurt.

Nothing else about enemies, weapons, shooting or scoring is changed.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Takes about 5 minutes the first time. Steps 1 and 2 are needed only once, for all MelonLoader mods.

[b]Where is my game folder?[/b] In Steam, right-click [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone[/i] →
[i]Manage[/i] → [i]Browse local files[/i]. It's the folder with [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] in it.

[b]1. Install MelonLoader[/b] (the mod loader)
Download the installer from [url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader releases[/url],
run it, pick [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] in your game folder, and install [b]version 0.7.3 or newer[/b].
[b]Version 0.6 does not work[/b] with this game.

[b]2. Start the game once[/b], then quit.
A black console window opens next to the game: that's MelonLoader working. The first start takes
a minute or two, later ones are normal.

[b]3. Add the mod[/b]
Open the downloaded zip. Inside is a folder called [b]Mods[/b]. Drag that [b]Mods[/b] folder into your
game folder, and if Windows asks, choose to replace / merge.
You should end up with: [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone\Mods\HeavyMelee.dll[/i]

[b]4. Check it works[/b]
Start the game. The MelonLoader console lists [b]Heavy Melee v1.0.0[/b] among the loaded mods.

[b]Not working?[/b]
[list]
[*][b]The mod isn't in the console list:[/b] check the file isn't at [i]Mods\Mods\HeavyMelee.dll[/i] (a folder
too deep). Move it up so it sits directly in [i]Mods[/i].
[*][b]No console window at all:[/b] MelonLoader isn't installed on this game. Repeat step 1 and make
sure you picked [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i].
[*][b]Errors right at startup:[/b] you likely have MelonLoader 0.6. Install 0.7.3 or newer.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings (optional)[/b][/size]
[spoiler]
After the first launch, edit [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i], section [b][HeavyMelee][/b]:
[code]
Enabled                = true   master switch
WeaponHits             = true   guns and bows hit like melee weapons
DownedHits             = true   hits on a stunned or downed enemy do damage
KeepDown               = true   hits keep a downed enemy on the ground
KeepDownSeconds        = 2.0    how long after your last hit they stay down
KnockDownGettingUp     = true   a hit while they get up knocks them back down
KnockoutOnGround       = true   any body part can finish a downed enemy
FistDamageMultiplier   = 2.0    fist damage, x the game's melee damage (10)
WeaponDamageMultiplier = 3.0    weapon damage, x the game's melee damage (10)
MaxDamagePerHit        = 50     most damage one hit can do (enemies have 100); 0 = no limit
MinPunchSpeed          = 3.0    m/s a fist must move to count
MinWeaponSpeed         = 3.0    m/s a weapon must move to count
OpenPalmNoHit          = true   an open hand (grip not squeezed) doesn't hit
FistGrip               = 0.5    how far to squeeze grip for a fist (0-1)
OpenPalmStrikeSpeed    = 8.0    m/s at which an open hand hits anyway; 0 = never
IgnoreKickedObjects    = true   downed enemies aren't hurt by objects they push themselves
HitCooldown            = 0.3    seconds between hits on the same enemy (one swing = one hit)
DebugLog               = false  log every hit to the MelonLoader console
[/code]
Head hits do 5x and torso hits 2x before the MaxDamagePerHit limit, as in the game.
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works alongside my other mods ([url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/22]Better Bow[/url],
Knee Shot Stun, Fire Selector). Knives, arrows and the nunchucks keep the game's own damage. May conflict with
other mods that change melee damage.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\HeavyMelee.dll[/i].
