[size=5][b]VR Holster Customization[/b][/size]

Move the game's [b]hip, knife and back holsters[/b] to suit your reach. [b]Grab an empty holster's position point with grip + trigger, then drag the holster by hand[/b]—even out in front of you. Fine-tune positions live in Mod Settings. Keep the [b]original game colours[/b] or choose another colour. And [b]carry a katana or knife on your back[/b]: it hangs on your shoulder, comes out with a grip press, and flies back to your back after you throw it.

[b]Required:[/b] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/28]Mod Settings[/url] — install the latest version. This is a hard dependency, not an optional menu.

[size=4][b]Grab and move holsters by hand[/b][/size]
This is the quickest way to place a holster where your hand naturally reaches: find its highlighted point, hold [b]grip + trigger[/b] to grab it, drag it into position, and release to save.
[list]
[*]Open [b]Mod Settings[/b] from the phone tile or Ctrl+M.
[*]Find [b]VR Holsters: move by hand[/b] and enable [b]Move empty holsters by hand[/b].
[*][b]Empty your hand and the holster.[/b] Bring your palm within about [b]12 cm[/b] of its position point; a blue marker identifies the nearest empty slot.
[*]At that point, hold [b]grip + trigger together for two seconds[/b]. Amber and small vibrations mean keep holding. Green and a stronger vibration mean you have grabbed the holster and it is ready to move.
[*][b]Drag the holster with your hand[/b] while keeping both buttons held. Release either button to save its new position.
[*]Disable adjustment when you are finished.
[/list]
Approach before pressing the buttons. Moving away during the initial hold cancels it; release and try again.
Positions move in 1 cm steps, normally within ±50 cm of the original position. Only one slot moves at a time.

This gesture moves the [b]six game holsters[/b]: left/right pistol, knife and back slots. Mod-item back slots use their separate settings.

[size=4][b]Fine-tune with the board[/b][/size]
Under [b]VR Holster Customization: positions[/b], each holster has height, left/right and forward/back controls, labelled in centimetres. Positive values move up, right or forward; negative values move in the opposite direction. [b]All holsters: height[/b] shifts all six together.

The actual sockets move with their highlights, so the grab positions follow your settings. Changes apply live and survive scene changes and restarts. Zero per-slot offsets leave the original position, plus any All holsters height adjustment.

Grip Mod Settings' top bar to move or tilt the board near your holsters, then point and press trigger to adjust from a distance. [b]Reset[/b] beside a position restores that parameter's declared default.

[size=4][b]Katana and knives on your back[/b][/size]
The game's [b]katanas and knives[/b] fit the two back slots, one blade per side.
[list]
[*][b]Put one away:[/b] take a blade in your hand, bring it over a shoulder (your hand buzzes over a free slot) and let go slowly. A fast release is a throw and stays a throw.
[*][b]Draw it:[/b] reach over the shoulder and press grip, the same way as any back-slot item.
[*][b]How it hangs:[/b] by its grip end with the flat of the blade against your back. Tilt, lean, spin and how far the grip hangs are adjustable in Mod Settings (BladeTiltDeg, BladeLeanDeg, BladeSpinDeg, BladeGripCm). Position and distance are shared with the other back-slot items.
[*][b]Solid only when drawn:[/b] a blade on your back is non-solid, so it never catches on walls or enemies, and it is solid again the moment you draw it.
[*][b]It comes back:[/b] a blade you drew from your back returns to your back after a throw or a drop, to its own side if free, otherwise to the other side. A blade from the belt holster or the wall uses the game's own return.
[*][b]How fast it returns:[/b] a thrown or dropped knife or katana returns on its own. By default it comes back 1 second after it sticks in or kills an enemy and 5 seconds after it lands on the floor or a wall (the game's own delay is 10). Change both in Mod Settings (InEnemySeconds, OnGroundSeconds); 0 leaves the game's timer alone.
[*][b]Carried into contracts:[/b] the blades on your back are saved in The Range and come with you into a contract, and are restored after a checkpoint retry.
[*][b]Sounds:[/b] holstering and drawing in a back slot plays a holster sound (HolsterSounds, on by default). HolsterInSound and HolsterOutSound let you pick other game sounds.
[*]Choose which blades fit with [b]BackBlades[/b]: [i]All[/i] (default), [i]Katana[/i] (katanas only) or [i]Off[/i]. Arrows and pens are never accepted.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Holster colours[/b][/size]
The [b]Color[/b] setting defaults to [b]Default[/b], which preserves the game's original normal and hover colours. It does not use your personal saved colour as its default.
Cycle colours in Mod Settings, or enter a custom [b]#RRGGBB[/b] value in the cfg. Reset returns to original game colours. Existing red/custom configurations stay custom until you reset them.

[size=4][b]Loadout saving[/b][/size]
Placing a gun or knife in a holster [b]in The Range[/b] schedules a loadout save about a second later. Contract holster activity does not trigger this automatic save, so putting a knife away while a pistol is drawn does not overwrite your prepared loadout with an empty pistol slot. Blades and registered mod-item back slots also save in The Range and restore their prepared items after a same-scene checkpoint retry, including Weapon Framework's crowbar.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[b]Find the game folder:[/b] Steam → right-click [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone[/i] → [i]Manage[/i] → [i]Browse local files[/i].

[b]1.[/b] Install [url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader[/url] for [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i]. Tested with [b]0.7.3[/b]; 0.6.x does not work.
[b]2.[/b] Start the game once, then quit.
[b]3.[/b] Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/28]the latest Mod Settings[/url] if you don't have it yet. This download contains VR Holster Customization only.
Extract the ZIP's [b]Mods[/b] folder into the game folder. You should have:
[code]
Mods\VRHolsterCustomization.dll
Mods\ModSettings.dll
[/code]
[b]4.[/b] Start the game. The MelonLoader console lists [b]VR Holster Customization v0.3.14[/b] among the loaded mods, along with Mod Settings.

[b]Troubleshooting[/b]
[list]
[*]A missing ModSettings dependency means [i]ModSettings.dll[/i] is absent from the game's Mods folder. Install the required mod.
[*]No adjustment marker? Enable move-by-hand mode, empty the slot and your hand, release both buttons, then approach again.
[*]Position controls remain available even when move-by-hand mode is off.
[*]Do not nest the DLLs under [i]Mods\Mods[/i].
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
[spoiler]
Use Mod Settings to change these live. The same entries are stored in [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i].
[code]
[VRHolsters]
DebugLog = false
BeltFollowsHead = true
BeltFreezeInMenu = true
BeltDeadZoneDeg = 30
BeltTurnSeconds = 0.1
BeltCenterSeconds = 1

[VRHolsters_Adjustment]
Enabled = false
MirrorPairs = true

[VRHolsters_Positions]
AllUpCm = 0
[/code]
For each of LeftHip, RightHip, LeftKnife, RightKnife, LeftBack and RightBack:
[code]
<slot>UpCm = 0
<slot>AlongCm = 0
<slot>ForwardCm = 0
[/code]
Replace <slot> with the slot name when editing the cfg, for example LeftHipUpCm.
[code]
[VRHolsters_Visual]
Color = "Default"

[VRHolsters_BackSlots]
BackHolsters = true
OutCm = -13
UpCm = 0
BackCm = 7
DropCm = 30
TiltDeg = 20
LeanDeg = 10
SpinDeg = 0
SnapCm = 30
DrawCm = 20
SavedBackHolsters = ""
BackBlades = "All"
BladeGripCm = 0
BladeTiltDeg = 35
BladeLeanDeg = 10
BladeSpinDeg = 0
HolsterSounds = true
HolsterInSound = "EQUIPTact_Equipment Metal Buckle Chain Jangle Latch Flap Belts 03_ESM_SG"
HolsterOutSound = "S_WEP_Knife_Attack_01"

[VRHolsters_KnifeReturn]
InEnemySeconds = 1
OnGroundSeconds = 5
[/code]
BackSlots controls supported mod items: position in centimetres, rotation in degrees, snap/draw distance and saved contents. BackBlades (All, Katana or Off) chooses which of the game's blades fit the back slots; the Blade* values set how they hang. HolsterSounds, HolsterInSound and HolsterOutSound set the holster sounds (blank = a gun's click). InEnemySeconds and OnGroundSeconds set how long a thrown knife or katana stays in an enemy or on the ground before it returns to the back slot (0 = the game's own 10 s timer). SavedBackHolsters is managed automatically; do not edit it during play.
BeltFollowsHead turns the hip and knife holsters with your head so they stay in front of you (the dead zone, turn time and center time tune how quickly); BeltFreezeInMenu keeps them still while the Mod Settings board is open, so you can set them up. MirrorPairs moves the other side's hip or knife holster to the mirrored spot when you move one by hand.
No personal cfg is included in the download.
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[b]Mod Settings is required.[/b] Weapon Framework and Daredevil are optional. With those installed, this mod also provides docking and back-slot support for registered mod items such as the test crowbar and Billy Clubs. Their back-slot positions are separate from the six game holsters' move-by-hand controls.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\VRHolsterCustomization.dll[/i]. Game holster position and colour overrides stop applying. Saved cfg values remain.
Weapon Framework versions that depend on this DLL must also be removed or updated. Keep Mod Settings if other installed mods require it.
