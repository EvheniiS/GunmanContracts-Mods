[size=5][b]Fire Selector[/b][/size]

Automatic weapons get a [b]fire selector[/b]: switch between [b]full auto[/b], [b]3-round burst[/b] and
[b]single shot[/b] without letting go of the gun.

[size=4][b]How to use[/b][/size]
Hold the gun normally. With your [b]other hand[/b] (on the foregrip or free), [b]hold X[/b] (left hand)
or [b]A[/b] (right hand) for about a third of a second. Each hold moves one step:
[b]Automatic → 3-round burst → Single → Automatic[/b].
Your hand vibrates to tell you which mode you're in:
[list]
[*][b]1 short pulse[/b]: single shot
[*][b]3 short pulses[/b]: 3-round burst
[*][b]1 long buzz[/b]: automatic
[/list]

[size=4][b]How the modes fire[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Single:[/b] one shot per trigger pull, as fast as the gun can cycle.
[*][b]3-round burst:[/b] three shots at the gun's own automatic fire rate. The burst finishes even if
you let go of the trigger, and stops early if the magazine runs dry.
[*][b]Automatic:[/b] unchanged, fires until you let go.
[/list]
Each weapon type [b]remembers[/b] the mode you picked, also after restarting the game.

[size=4][b]Your buttons keep working[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The gun hand's A / X still drops the magazine instantly.[/b] The mod doesn't touch it.
[*]In the game, a [b]quick tap[/b] of A / X with the hand on the foregrip also drops the magazine,
or toggles the flashlight on guns with the light attachment. That still works: a tap does
exactly what it did before, only when you [i]let go[/i] of the button instead of when you press
it. A [b]hold[/b] switches the fire mode instead, so switching never costs you a magazine.
[*]A hand holding something else (a knife, a magazine, the phone, an arrow) keeps its buttons.
[*]Only guns that are automatic in the game get the selector. Pistols, shotguns and the bow are
not affected. Enemy weapons are not affected.
[/list]

The three modes are already built into the game's VR framework (HurricaneVR). The game just
never lets you switch, so this mod only switches the mode. Shooting itself is the game's own code.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader 0.7.x[/url] (tested with 0.7.3)

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install MelonLoader on [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] with the official installer.
[*]Extract the zip into the game folder (the one with [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i]).
[b]FireSelector.dll[/b] ends up in [i]Mods[/i]. Or copy it there by hand.
[*]Start the game. The first launch with MelonLoader takes a minute or two.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings (optional)[/b][/size]
After the first launch, edit [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i], section [b][FireSelector][/b]:
[code]
Enabled       = true   the fire selector
HoldSeconds   = 0.35   how long to hold A / X before the mode switches
Haptics       = true   vibrate to show the new mode
RememberModes = true   each weapon type keeps its mode, also after a restart
SavedModes    = ""     the remembered modes (written by the mod)
DebugLog      = false  log each gun and every switch to the MelonLoader console
[/code]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works alongside my other mods ([url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/22]Better Bow[/url],
Knee Shot Stun). Better Bow's dagger grip also uses A / X, but only while that hand holds an
arrow, so the two never clash.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\FireSelector.dll[/i]. Your guns go back to their normal mode.
