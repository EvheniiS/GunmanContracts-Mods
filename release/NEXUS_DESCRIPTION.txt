[size=5][b]Arrow Grab Assist[/b][/size]

Grab the next arrow on the [b]first[/b] grip press, every time, even when you're firing fast.
Plus: [b]one arrow now detonates an explosive barrel[/b] instead of two.

[size=4][b]The problem[/b][/size]
When you shoot fast, you often have to press grip twice to get the next arrow off the string.
The game only grabs on the exact frame you press grip, and while you hold grip the hand can't
pick up anything new. If your hand reaches the string a moment after you press, the press is
ignored and you have to release and press again.

In testing, the hand was usually only [b]2–6 cm[/b] from the string when the game ignored the
press. The grab zone isn't too small; early presses were being thrown away.

[size=4][b]What the mod does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Grip-press buffer:[/b] if you press grip near the bow string (within 15 cm) while holding the
bow in your other hand and the game misses it, the mod completes the grab through the game's own
grab function. Arrows, ammo and haptics behave exactly as normal. It only affects the bow
string; everything else grabs as before.
[*][b]Explosive barrels:[/b] arrows hit breakables with their raw damage (bullets and explosions get
a multiplier, arrows don't), so a barrel needed two arrows. Now an arrow hit on an
[i]explosive[/i] barrel always detonates it. Knives, bullets and non-explosive objects are unchanged.
[/list]

Tested: 55 of 55 fast draws grabbed on the first press.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader 0.7.x[/url] (tested with 0.7.3)

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install MelonLoader on [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] with the official installer.
[*]Put [b]ArrowGrabAssist.dll[/b] into the game's [i]Mods[/i] folder.
[*]Start the game. The first launch with MelonLoader takes a minute or two.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings (optional)[/b][/size]
After the first launch, edit [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i], section [b][ArrowGrabAssist][/b]:
[code]
Enabled                        = true   master switch
GrabRadius                     = 0.15   metres from hand to string
PressBufferSeconds             = 0.35   how late after pressing grip the grab can still happen
RetryArrowSpawn                = true   spawn the arrow if the string was grabbed too soon after a shot
ExplosiveArrowsDetonateBarrels = true   one arrow detonates an explosive barrel
DebugLog                       = false  log every assisted grab / barrel hit to the console
[/code]

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\ArrowGrabAssist.dll[/i].

Tested on game version 0.3.1.0, MelonLoader 0.7.3, Quest 3 via Virtual Desktop.
