[size=5][b]Grab Fix[/b][/size]

Make pickups more forgiving with [b]finger and palm targeting[/b], a [b]short grip-press buffer[/b],
and [b]easier catches[/b] for items you just released. [b]Holstered items keep their original grab zones[/b].

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Aim with your fingers or your palm:[/b] keeps the game's finger-aimed distance pickup and adds palm-aimed detection. Choose Finger in settings if you only want the original aiming direction.
[*][b]More forgiving nearby pickups:[/b] widens the local pickup area to cover both the finger side and the palm. The default minimum radius is 12 cm.
[*][b]Wider distance targeting:[/b] distance pickup detectors are 20% wider by default, without extending their reach.
[*][b]Early grip presses count:[/b] press grip just before an item becomes available and keep it held. The mod remembers that press for 0.30 seconds and retries eligible local or distance pickups.
[*][b]Catch a released item:[/b] an item released from either hand stays eligible for extra catch assistance for 15 seconds. A fresh grip press gives you a 0.60-second catch window when its collider surface comes within 18 cm of your palm.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Holsters and deliberate grabs[/b][/size]
[b]Enemies need your palm on them:[/b] standing, stunned and downed enemies are only grabbed when your palm is within 10 cm of their body, and never from a distance. A punch that brushes a face or a body at your feet no longer grabs it.
Holstered, socketed and wall-mounted items use their original grab zones, so the wider pickup area and palm targeting do not extend their reach. Recently holstered items are excluded from catch assistance. [b]Holstered items stay drawable even when another mod makes them non-solid[/b] (for example Daredevil 1.1.1 clubs on your belt): the reach check counts their colliders either way.
You still press grip deliberately; holding it indefinitely does not keep retrying. The mod uses the game's normal grab checks and distance-grab controls. It changes pickup detection, not how a held item sits in your hand.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Takes about 5 minutes the first time. Steps 1 and 2 are needed only once, for all MelonLoader mods.

[b]Where is my game folder?[/b] In Steam, right-click [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone[/i] →
[i]Manage[/i] → [i]Browse local files[/i]. It's the folder with [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] in it.

[b]1. Install MelonLoader[/b] (the mod loader)
Download the installer from [url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader releases[/url],
run it, pick [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i] in your game folder, and install [b]version 0.7.3 or newer[/b].
[b]Version 0.6 does not work[/b] with this game.

[b]2. Start the game once[/b], then quit.
A black console window opens next to the game: that's MelonLoader working. The first start takes
a minute or two, later ones are normal.

[b]3. Add the mod[/b]
Open the downloaded zip. Inside is a folder called [b]Mods[/b]. Drag that [b]Mods[/b] folder into your
game folder, and if Windows asks, choose to replace / merge.
You should end up with: [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone\Mods\GrabFix.dll[/i]

[b]4. Check it works[/b]
Start the game. The MelonLoader console lists [b]Grab Fix v1.1.2[/b] among the loaded mods.

[size=4][b]Not working?[/b][/size]
[spoiler]
[list]
[*][b]The mod isn't in the console list:[/b] check the file isn't at [i]Mods\Mods\GrabFix.dll[/i] (a folder
too deep). Move it up so it sits directly in [i]Mods[/i].
[*][b]No console window at all:[/b] MelonLoader isn't installed on this game. Repeat step 1 and make
sure you picked [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i].
[*][b]Errors right at startup:[/b] you likely have MelonLoader 0.6. Install 0.7.3 or newer.
[/list]
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Settings (optional)[/b][/size]
[spoiler]
After the first launch, edit [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i], section [b][GrabFix][/b]:
[code]
Enabled                = true    master switch; off restores original pickup geometry
GrabDirection          = "Both"  Both = finger + palm; Finger = original aim, still widened
NearGrabRadius         = 0.12    minimum local radius in metres; Both may grow it further; max 0.25
PalmOffset             = 0.03    reach in front of the palm in Both mode, metres (0-0.08)
DistanceGrabWidth      = 1.2     distance detector width multiplier (1-2); no extra reach
BufferGripPress        = true    remember an early grip press while grip stays held
GripBufferSeconds      = 0.30    local/distance grip buffer in real seconds (0-0.6)
RecentReleaseAssist    = true    extra catch tolerance for recently released loose items
RecentReleaseRadius    = 0.18    palm-to-item surface catch distance, metres (0.05-0.3)
RecentReleaseSeconds   = 15      eligibility after release in real seconds (0-60)
CatchBufferSeconds     = 0.60    fresh grip press catch window in real seconds (0-1)
EnemyGrabRadius        = 0.10    palm-to-enemy distance for grabbing enemies, metres (0-0.25; 0 = no limit)
DebugLog               = false   log assisted pickups and detector setup
[/code]
Or change these live in-VR with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/28][b]Mod Settings[/b][/url] (optional) — no restart, no text editor.
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[b]Daredevil 1.1.1 or newer requires Grab Fix 1.1.2 or newer.[/b] With an older Grab Fix, a club on your belt cannot be drawn. Get [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/gunmancontractsstandalone/mods/40]Daredevil[/url] and Grab Fix from their pages at the same time.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\GrabFix.dll[/i].
