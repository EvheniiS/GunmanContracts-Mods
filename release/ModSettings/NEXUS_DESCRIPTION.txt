[size=5][b]Mod Settings[/b][/size]

Change your installed mods' settings from a [b]movable VR board[/b], using [b]pointing hands and a fingertip laser[/b] or direct touch. Each setting shows whether it is [b]default or changed[/b], with its own Reset button.
[b]Playing flat?[/b] Ctrl+M opens a mouse-operated menu on screen.

[size=4][b]How to use in VR[/b][/size]
Open the phone and press the [b]Mod Settings[/b] tile, or press [b]Ctrl+M[/b].
[list]
[*][b]Point and click:[/b] aim an empty hand at the board and press trigger. A laser starts at your fingertip, and the hand uses the game's menu pointing pose. Works from up to 4 metres away.
[*][b]Touch:[/b] press a control from the front with either index fingertip.
[*][b]Move and tilt:[/b] grip the top bar, move or rotate your hand, then release. You can grab it nearby or aim the laser at the bar and grip. Put it beside your holsters or near the floor while configuring them.
[*]The board follows stick movement and snap turns. Close and reopen it to bring it back in front of you.
[*]Use [b]< / >[/b] at the top to switch mods and [b]Up / Down[/b] to scroll settings.
[*]Press [b]List[/b] (or the mod's name) for a grid of every mod, alphabetical; press one to jump straight to it.
[*]Select a setting's name for its description. Switches toggle; numbers have small and large steps; choices and colours cycle.
[/list]
The board is 72 × 56 cm at scale 1. Held weapons keep their own hand pose. Release the opening gesture before using the controls.

[size=4][b]Defaults and resetting[/b][/size]
Every VR row shows [b]DEFAULT[/b] or [b]CHANGED[/b] and its declared default value. Changed names are yellow, and a [b]Reset[/b] button beside the value restores that individual setting. The desktop menu also has per-row reset controls.

Defaults come from each mod's code, not from your first launch or your personal configuration. Mod Settings compares the current value with that declared default. Mod-managed saved state is protected from reset. Other text that cannot be edited here can still be reset when it has changed; edit custom text in [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i].

[size=4][b]After a mod update[/b][/size]
MelonLoader keeps your old values when a mod update changes a default, so a better new default would never reach you. Mod Settings remembers each setting's default and, when an update changes one:
[list]
[*]a setting you never changed moves to the new default by itself;
[*]a setting you changed keeps your value, and you are asked.
[/list]
The first time you open the menu afterwards, a small popup names the updated mod and offers [b]Use new[/b], [b]Keep mine[/b], [b]Review[/b] (one row per setting, with Undo) or [b]Later[/b]. It also tells you once if a mod's settings were reset because the settings file was deleted. The first run only records the defaults.

[size=4][b]Flat mode (no headset)[/b][/size]
Press [b]Ctrl+M[/b], click the controls with the mouse, and use the mouse wheel to scroll. [b]X[/b] or Ctrl+M closes the menu. While open, the cursor is free and mouse look and shooting stop. Walking still works; [b]the game is not paused[/b].

[size=4][b]When changes apply[/b][/size]
Changes update the mod's MelonPreferences entry immediately and are saved about a second later. Each mod decides when it uses the new value: some update live, others need a level reload or restart. [b]*[/b] marks descriptions that mention a game restart.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[b]Find the game folder:[/b] Steam → right-click [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone[/i] → [i]Manage[/i] → [i]Browse local files[/i]. This is the folder containing [i]GunmanContracts.exe[/i].

[b]1.[/b] Install [url=https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases]MelonLoader[/url] for this game. Tested with [b]0.7.3[/b]; 0.6.x does not work.
[b]2.[/b] Start the game once, then quit so MelonLoader can prepare its files.
[b]3.[/b] Extract the ZIP's [b]Mods[/b] folder into the game folder and merge/replace when prompted.
The DLL must be at [i]Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone\Mods\ModSettings.dll[/i].
[b]4.[/b] Start the game. The console should show [b]Mod Settings v1.3.0[/b] among the loaded mods.

[b]Troubleshooting[/b]
[list]
[*]Do not put the DLL in [i]Mods\Mods[/i]; it belongs directly in [i]Mods[/i].
[*]If a mission item occupies the phone tile's slot, the tile moves to another empty slot. Ctrl+M also opens the menu.
[*]If Ctrl+M does nothing in flat mode, give the game window keyboard focus.
[*]If the board is out of reach, close and reopen it. Check that your pointing hand is empty.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
[spoiler]
Under [b]Mod Settings[/b] on the board, or [b][ModSettings][/b] in [i]UserData\MelonPreferences.cfg[/i]:
[code]
PhoneTile = true       show the phone tile
OpenKey = "M"          Ctrl + this key opens/closes the menu
PanelDistance = 0.4    opening distance in metres
PanelScale = 1         board size: 72 × 56 cm at 1; applies on next opening
DebugLog = false       menu diagnostics and phone-layout logging
[/code]
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Lists visible MelonPreferences settings from installed mods automatically; other mods do not need a special integration.
[b]VR Holster Customization requires Mod Settings.[/b] Its holster adjustment pages work particularly well with the movable board.
Mod Settings itself does not require VR Holsters, Weapon Framework or Daredevil.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]Mods\ModSettings.dll[/i]. Saved preference values remain in your cfg.
Mods that require Mod Settings, including VR Holster Customization, also need to be removed or will have a missing dependency.

[size=4][b]Reset a whole section[/b][/size]
[b]Reset section[/b] at the bottom restores all editable settings in the selected config section, including settings on other pages. The [b]Reset[/b] button beside each value still resets only that setting. Hidden entries and mod-managed saved state are excluded. Available in VR and flat mode.
