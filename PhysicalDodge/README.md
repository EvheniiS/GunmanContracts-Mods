# Physical Dodge

Tested: 0.4.2

Part of [Gunman Contracts Mods](../README.md). Enemies aim where you were a moment ago, so standing still gets you hit and stepping, leaning, ducking or moving makes their shots go past (a short buzz and a brief slow motion when one would have hit).
